Frequently asked questions

Do you charge a fee?

Consultation is free. We will tell you about any lender charges before you apply.

Will enquiring affect my credit score?

No. We do not run a credit check just to enquire. A lender checks it only when you formally apply.

How long does approval take?

It depends on the lender and loan type. Personal loans can be quick. Home loans usually take a few days to a few weeks.

Who lends the money?

Banks and NBFCs. We are consultants and do not lend ourselves. All loans are subject to lender approval.

Can I transfer my existing loan?

Yes. If another lender offers a lower rate, a balance transfer can reduce your EMI. We compare the savings against the fees.

What affects my interest rate?

Your credit score, income, loan amount, tenure and the property or business profile.
